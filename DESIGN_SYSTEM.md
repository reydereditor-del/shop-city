# SHOP CITY — Design direction (provisional)

## Ambition
Original, editorial fashion experience that feels art-directed, not generic. A beautiful website alone is insufficient: the garment, fit, size, price, and buying path must remain clear. References may inform principles, never copy layouts/assets.

## Reference roles
- Entire Studios: impactful, restrained campaign focus.
- Jacquemus: campaign-led storytelling.
- Represent: commercial usability and curated collections.
- COS: clean gallery, fit/care clarity.
- Acne Studios: distinctive editorial collection routes.

## Three concepts for visual comparison (NOT yet selected)
A. **FRAME / NOIR** — dark editorial entry, quiet off-white catalog, sharp type; cinematic restraint.
B. **STUDIO / OBJECT** — light neutral immersive fabric/detail photography, asymmetrical layout, slow intentional reveals.
C. **CITY / EDIT** — local Tashkent-inspired street-fashion campaign, layered photography, kinetic but useful editorial-to-catalog links.

Each concept must present: desktop homepage above/below fold; mobile first screen; catalog/product card; product detail; representative type, spacing, navigation and motion story. Judge originality, brand suitability, accessibility, loading, product clarity, and maintainability.

## Baseline interaction rules
- Motion supports narrative transitions, hierarchy, product discovery. Never delay visible CTA, product name, price or accessibility focus.
- Prototype GSAP ScrollTrigger only after static layout; measure performance and respect prefers-reduced-motion.
- Optional experimental 3D prototype must demonstrate actual value (e.g. material construction); no gratuitous scene or 3D for every SKU.
- Mobile deliberately composed, not a desktop compression. Keyboard, touch, and reduced-motion paths are mandatory.
- Catalog/admin have stable, predictable UI. Separate storefront art direction from operational simplicity.

## Unresolved before implementation
Actual Shop City logo, category mix, languages, brand permission, suitable media and licensing, reference device performance. Sample images must be explicitly marked and legally sourced. All visual decisions remain proposals until reviewed.
